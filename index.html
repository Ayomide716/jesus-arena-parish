You are welcome here

What to expect on your first visit

Come as you are. Our services are warm, Bible-centred and full of joyful worship. If you let us know you are coming, someone from our welcome team will be waiting to meet you at the door.

Easy to find

Parking is free and our ushers will show you where to go. Look for the welcome desk just inside the main entrance.

Rooted in the Word

Every service centres on Scripture and practical teaching you can carry into your week, your work and your home.

Room for the children

Children’s church and the teens’ class run alongside the main service, so your little ones are cared for and taught at their own level.

About our parish

One family, called to lift Jesus up

Jesus Arena Parish is part of the Redeemed Christian Church of God, a worldwide family of churches. We exist to make heaven, to take as many people with us as possible, and to hold firmly to holiness, prayer and the teaching of God’s Word.

From our first gathering to today, the heart of this parish has stayed the same: a place where strangers become family, where the hurting find hope, and where every believer is equipped to serve.

What we believe

  • The Bible is the inspired and infallible Word of God.
  • Salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone.
  • The Holy Spirit empowers every believer for holy living and service.
  • Prayer, worship and fellowship shape the life of the church.
  • Every member has a gift to be used for the good of the body.

Get involved

Ministries and fellowships

There is a place for everyone to grow and to serve. Speak to any usher on Sunday, or send us a message, and we will connect you.

Children’s Church

Bible lessons, songs and activities for ages 3–12, running during the main Sunday service.

Teens & Youth

A safe, lively space for young people to ask honest questions and build a faith that is truly their own.

Choir & Worship Team

Singers and instrumentalists leading the congregation into the presence of God. Rehearsals hold weekly.

Women’s Fellowship (Good Women)

Prayer, mentoring and practical support for women in every stage of life.

Men’s Fellowship

Building men who lead their homes, their work and their community with integrity.

Evangelism & Outreach

Taking the good news beyond our walls through visitation, street outreach and community care.

Ushering & Welcome Team

The first smile every visitor sees — serving with order, warmth and hospitality.

Media & Technical

Sound, live streaming, photography and design, so the message reaches far beyond the building.

Prayer Band

Interceding for the parish, our families and our nation. Join us for our weekly prayer meeting.
